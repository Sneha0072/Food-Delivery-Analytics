# Food-Delivery-Analytics
This project analyzes food delivery operations using Python, MySQL, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly. The objective is to understand customer behavior, restaurant performance, order trends, payment preferences, and delivery efficiency to support data-driven business decisions.
# 🍔 Food Delivery Analytics - Final Business Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes food delivery operations using **Python, MySQL, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly**. The objective is to understand customer behavior, restaurant performance, order trends, payment preferences, and delivery efficiency to support data-driven business decisions.

---

# 📈 Key Business Insights

### 💰 Revenue Analysis

- The platform generated significant revenue from **10,000 customer orders**.
- The average order value indicates healthy customer spending patterns.
- A small percentage of high-value orders contributed substantially to the overall revenue.
- Delivered orders generated the majority of the platform's revenue.

---

### 👥 Customer Analysis

- The platform serves **1,000 unique customers** across multiple cities.
- A small group of loyal customers contributed a large portion of total revenue.
- Customers aged **20–40 years** placed the highest number of orders.
- Both male and female customers actively use the platform, indicating balanced customer engagement.

---

### 🍽️ Restaurant Performance

- A few restaurants consistently generated higher revenue than others.
- Popular restaurants received significantly more customer orders.
- Highly rated restaurants generally attracted more customers.
- North Indian, Fast Food, and Chinese cuisines were among the most frequently ordered categories.

---

### 🌆 City Performance

- Revenue generation varied across different cities.
- Metropolitan cities contributed the highest share of total sales.
- Some cities showed lower order volumes, indicating opportunities for future expansion.

---

### 💳 Payment Analysis

- UPI emerged as the most preferred payment method.
- Digital payment methods (UPI, Wallet, and Card) dominated over Cash.
- The payment success rate remained consistently high, indicating reliable payment processing.

---

### 🚴 Delivery Analysis

- Bike deliveries accounted for the majority of completed deliveries.
- Delivery partners maintained good customer ratings.
- Average delivery time remained within an acceptable range.
- Faster deliveries were generally associated with higher customer satisfaction.

---

### ⭐ Customer Feedback Analysis

- Most customer ratings ranged between **4 and 5 stars**.
- Positive feedback commonly mentioned:
  - Fast delivery
  - Good food quality
  - Proper packaging
- Negative feedback mainly focused on:
  - Cold food
  - Delayed delivery
  - Packaging issues

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Promote Top Restaurants
Increase promotional campaigns for top-performing restaurants to maximize revenue.

### 2. Reward Loyal Customers
Provide loyalty rewards and personalized offers to high-value customers to improve customer retention.

### 3. Encourage Digital Payments
Promote UPI and Wallet payments through cashback offers and exclusive discounts.

### 4. Improve Delivery Operations
Increase the number of delivery partners in cities with higher delivery times to improve customer satisfaction.

### 5. Monitor Restaurant Quality
Work closely with low-rated restaurants to improve food quality and packaging standards.

### 6. Expand into Growing Markets
Focus expansion efforts on high-performing cities while running targeted marketing campaigns in lower-performing regions.

### 7. Use Customer Feedback
Continuously analyze customer reviews to identify recurring issues and improve service quality.
